A few helpful details

Before you
get in touch.

Do I need to book a DJ to get the photo booth?

No. The photo booth is its own booking. If you’d like music too, I’m a DJ and host as well, and I’m happy to quote both together.

How do guests get their photos?

Right at the booth. Guests send their photos to their own phone by text, email or AirDrop, so they can share them that night.

Do we get all the photos afterwards?

Yes. After the event you receive every image taken at the booth.

Can the photos match our theme?

Yes. Every booth includes custom branding, so the photos carry your name, date, logo or theme in your colours.

Can we get printed photos?

Yes. Add unlimited on-site prints and a dedicated attendant for $200. Otherwise the booth is fully digital.

Which package should we pick?

Most parties choose 3 hours. If guests are arriving over a long evening, or it’s a gala or holiday party, up to 5 hours means nobody misses it.

How do we book?

Send your date, the occasion and how long you’d like the booth through the form below. I’ll confirm availability and send an agreement. A 50% booking fee reserves your date.
